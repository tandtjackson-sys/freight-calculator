Understanding FedEx Dimensional Weight Calculations

FedEx assesses dimensional (DIM) weight on all Express, Ground, and International packages. DIM weight reflects package density—the amount of space a package occupies relative to its actual physical weight.

FedEx Imperial & Metric DIM Formulas

For standard imperial measurements (inches and pounds), calculate volume and divide by the standard 139 factor:

(Length × Width × Height) ÷ 139 = Dimensional Weight (lbs)

For metric measurements (centimeters and kilograms), use the metric divisor of 5000:

(Length × Width × Height in cm) ÷ 5000 = Dimensional Weight (kg)

FedEx Rounding & Surcharge Rules

FedEx rounds fractions of an inch to the next whole number (e.g., 10.1 inches becomes 11 inches). Always check package girth (Length + 2xWidth + 2xHeight) to avoid additional handling or oversize surcharges when shipping large, lightweight cartons.
