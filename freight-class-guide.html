What is Freight Class?

In Less-Than-Truckload (LTL) shipping, freight class is a standardized classification system created by the National Motor Freight Traffic Association (NMFTA). It categorizes commodities into 18 distinct classes ranging from Class 50 to Class 400 based on four key metrics: density, stowability, handling, and liability.

How Density Drives Freight Class

Density (measured in pounds per cubic foot) is the single most important factor. Heavier, compact freight takes up less volume relative to its weight, making it cheaper to transport per pound.
