Understanding DHL Express Volumetric Weight Calculations

DHL Express applies volumetric (dimensional) weight calculations to all international air express cargo. Volumetric weight reflects package density—calculating how much space a box occupies inside an aircraft cargo hold relative to its actual deadweight.

DHL Volumetric Weight Formulas (Metric & Imperial)

For international shipments using metric measurements (centimeters and kilograms), DHL uses the standard IATA volumetric divisor of 5000:

(Length × Width × Height in cm) ÷ 5000 = Volumetric Weight (kg)

For shipments measured in inches and pounds, DHL uses the imperial conversion divisor of 139:

(Length × Width × Height in inches) ÷ 139 = Dimensional Weight (lbs)

DHL Rounding & Oversize Surcharges

DHL rounds measurements up to the next whole unit prior to volume calculation. Packages exceeding 120 cm (47 inches) on any single side or weighing more than 70 kg (154 lbs) incur individual Non-Stackable or Over-Piece / Oversize surcharges.
